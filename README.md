## 🍎 About me

I'm a systems engineer with **20+ years on the Macintosh**, currently building and securing the Apple fleet at **Exelixis**. I've spent my career making Macs first-class citizens in regulated, security-conscious environments, and sharing what I build with the Mac admin community along the way.

- 🔭 Currently working on **Jamf Pro** automation, endpoint security integrations, and **AWS EC2 Mac** workflows
- 🛡️ Focused on keeping macOS secure and compliant at scale: EDR, vulnerability management, and anti-ransomware tooling
- 🎤 Spoke at **JNUC** on the *Securing Macs in Any Environment* panel
- 🌏 Alumnus of Victoria University of Wellington
- 💬 Find me as **@jhbush** on the [MacAdmins Slack](https://www.macadmins.org). Ask me about Jamf extension attributes, AutoPkg, macOS security, or Mac fleet management
